THE VEIL - trying the Meshy characters (branch art/npc-meshy-trial-eddie-clerk)

1. Install Godot 4.3 (standard, not .NET): https://godotengine.org/download/archive/4.3-stable/
2. Unzip this project. The first time Godot opens it, it imports the assets (a few minutes).
3. To play the game with Eddie and the clerk replaced by the new models:
   - double-click PLAY_MESHY_TRIAL.bat (set the GODOT environment variable to your godot.exe first, or put godot.exe on PATH), or
   - run:  godot --path . -- meshy_npcs
   Without that argument the game is exactly as before.
   Eddie is in the game shop (customer service counter); the cashier stands at lane 4 in the early chapters (she is hidden from chapter 7 on).
4. To see all nine NPCs in the supermarket: PLAY_NPC_SUPERMARKET_TEST.bat
   (keys 1-9 pick an NPC, then T talk, B blink, S scared, A angry, W worried, N neutral). Your save file is backed up and restored by this test.
Everything about the characters: docs/HANDOFF_MESHY_CHARACTERS.md
