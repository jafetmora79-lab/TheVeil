THE VEIL - the Meshy characters now play ALL the characters (branch art/npc-meshy-trial-eddie-clerk)

1. Install Godot 4.3 (standard, not .NET): https://godotengine.org/download/archive/4.3-stable/
2. Unzip this project. The first time Godot opens it, it imports the assets (a few minutes).
3. Play: double-click PLAY_THE_VEIL.bat (set the GODOT environment variable to your godot.exe first, or put godot.exe on PATH), or run:  godot --path .
   Who plays whom (edit game/npc/meshy_npcs.gd, MAP): Protagonist = new protagonist, Eddie = eddie, Marcus = bearded man, Noah = teen, Ruth = elderly woman,
   Sarah = the Meshy Sarah, Lena = plaid-shirt woman, Frank = police cap, Officer = police officer, Cashier / survivor / extras = store clerk, Civilian_B = red-haired officer.
4. To play with the OLD characters instead: PLAY_WITH_OLD_NPCS.bat or  godot --path . -- old_npcs
5. To see all nine NPCs in the supermarket: godot --path . res://game/prototypes/npc_meshy/NpcSupermarketTest.tscn
   (keys 1-9 pick an NPC, then T talk, B blink, S scared, A angry, W worried, N neutral). Test scenes back up and restore your save file.
Everything about the characters: docs/HANDOFF_MESHY_CHARACTERS.md
