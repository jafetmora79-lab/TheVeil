# Pack 07 - Dimensional Creatures, Mist Tentacles & Supernatural Monsters

Supernatural creature collection for THE VEIL. Every model is generated by deterministic Python (numpy + Pillow)
in `Source_Scripts/`, exports to a skinned, animated GLB and imports into Godot 4.3 (Compatibility renderer).

**Validation covers structure, rigging, skinning maths and engine compatibility only. It does not prove visual
quality, and no screenshots, renders or videos were made.**

## Build and validate

Run from `THE_VEIL_ASSETS_V2/`:

```bash
python3 07_Creatures/Source_Scripts/build_creatures.py
```

```bash
python3 07_Creatures/Source_Scripts/validate_creatures.py --gltf-validator <dir with node_modules/gltf-validator> --godot <Godot_v4.3 binary> --godot-project <scratch project dir>
```

`build_creatures.py colossus` (or `mist`, `hound`, `skitter`, `shellcrawler`) rebuilds just one creature. The build
also writes `Documentation/CREATURE_MANIFEST.json` and adds the files to the browser gallery.

## Conventions

* Metres, +Y up. **Creatures face +Z**, the same as the Pack 06 characters (glTF / Godot model front), so one
  controller can drive both: `look_at(target, Vector3.UP, true)`. The origin is on the ground under the body.
* Custom skeleton per creature, **identity rest rotations**, joints placed by translation. Sockets are plain child
  nodes of joints; Godot imports them as extra bones (as in Pack 06).
* Animations are baked keyframes (rotation on every bone, plus translation on the body bone where it moves).
  Locomotion is **in place**; the walk/run speed is stored in the scene metadata (`walk_speed_mps` etc.).
* Every GLB has the textures embedded (for the browser gallery). In Godot the `.glb.import` remaps every material
  to its external `.tres` in `Shared_Materials/`, discards the embedded copies (no extracted PNGs), keeps every
  bone track (`remove_immutable_tracks=false`), sets the loop mode per clip, and creates a `RESET` animation from
  the rest pose.
* Prefixes: `CRT_*` models / scenes, `T_Crt_*` textures, `M_Crt_*` materials.
* Textures are pixel art with nearest filtering (`texture_filter = 2`). Albedo pixels are palette-locked: Pack 01
  master ramps + Pack 06 character ramps + the creature ramps in `cr_tex.CRT_RAMPS` (charcoal, desaturated purple,
  dark grey-green, muted bone, deep blue-black, wet inner tissue, and a pale "glow" ramp). Emission masks are
  black except for sparse pixels from the glow ramp (the validator caps emissive coverage at 15 %).
* Texel density: humans are about 64 px/m. The Colossus hide and the shared tentacle skin tile at **32 px/m**
  (128 px every 4 m) because the Colossus is seen from far away; its detail atlas (eyes, plates, maw, claws) is
  denser. The smaller creatures use one 256 px atlas each, close to the human density.
* Collision in the Godot scenes is simple and static: boxes / cylinders on a `CharacterBody3D` root. It does not
  follow the animation. There is no AI, damage, pathfinding or encounter logic anywhere in this pack.

## Creature 01 - The Colossus

`Colossus/CRT_Colossus.glb` - 62 bones (+3 sockets), 8 476 triangles, 3 materials, 8 clips.
Scene: `Godot_Scenes/CRT_Colossus.tscn`.

**Size:** about 13 m to the top of the back, 16.5 m to the top of the dorsal tentacle arch, 13 x 15 m footprint.

**Anatomy (no face, no arms, not upright):**

* A hunched dorsal mass, highest over a heavier left shoulder, with a hump on the right rear and a dorsal ridge.
  It ends in a heavy **hood** that hides the head.
* An **open rib cage** under it, with uneven ribs: 4 on the left, 3 plus a broken stub on the right. The ribs hold a
  **ventral sac** that hangs from a sinew stalk. A dim pale **core** hangs in the hollow between the two masses.
* The head is a lumpy **sensory mass** under the hood, with:
  * six pale orbs of different sizes, set asymmetrically;
  * two drooping sensory stalks;
  * a **vertical maw** that splits sideways between two hinged mandible plates.
* **Exactly four walking legs**, all different:
  * long sprawling forelegs: shoulder, a high lateral elbow, a long fluted stilt, then a hooked three-digit foot;
  * shorter hind legs: a forward knee, a backward hock, then a split hoof plate;
  * the left foreleg is the heaviest and the right hind leg the shortest;
  * every elbow / knee has overlapping joint plates and a bone spur.
* **Six tentacles**, each leaving the body through a puckered orifice:
  * two dorsal: one arches over the back, the other reaches out to the right;
  * two from the flanks of the rib cage;
  * two hanging from the sac.
* Overlapping bone / charcoal plates along the back, three gill-like openings on the right flank, and faint
  luminous cracks in the hide.

**Skeleton:**

* `Root > Body`, with these children:
  * `Chest > Head > Maw_L / Maw_R / Stalk_L_01-02 / Stalk_R_01-02`
  * `Rear`
  * `Belly`
* Legs `Leg_{FL,FR,HL,HR}_{Upper,Lower,Foot}`. The forelegs hang from `Chest`, the hind legs from `Rear`.
* Tentacles `T_DorsalL_01-08`, `T_DorsalR_01-07`, `T_FlankL_01-06`, `T_FlankR_01-06`, `T_VentA_01-06`,
  `T_VentB_01-05`.
* Sockets `Socket_Head`, `Socket_Core`, `Socket_DorsalTip`.

**Clips** (20 fps keys; the walk is in place at 1.0 m/s, 3 m stride, lateral-sequence gait, feet planted by IK):

| Clip | Length | Loop | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| `Idle_Breathe` | 4.0 s | yes | body rises / sinks, sac sways, maw breathes, slow tentacle drift, feet planted |
| `Walk_Heavy` | 4.0 s | yes | four-legged walk, body roll / bob, trailing tentacles, head counter-sway |
| `Tentacles_Sway` | 6.0 s | yes | idle body with strong tentacle writhing (layer it, or use it as an "agitated idle") |
| `Alert` | 2.4 s | no | rises on its legs, hood lifts and turns, maw opens, tentacles rise and spread; ends in a held pose |
| `Attack_TentacleStrike` | 2.4 s | no | wind-up, then the arching dorsal tentacle whips forward over the hood; impact at 1.05 s (`Markers/StrikeImpact`) |
| `Attack_Heavy` | 3.4 s | no | rears up on the hind legs and slams both forefeet down (impact at about 1.75 s) |
| `Hit_React` | 1.2 s | no | lateral jolt, head recoil, tentacles flinch, feet stay planted |
| `Death_Collapse` | 4.5 s | no | stagger, then the legs splay and buckle; the body sinks about 4 m, the hood droops and the tentacles go limp |

All tentacle poses pass a deterministic ground guard, so tentacles bend up instead of passing through the ground.

**Materials:**

* `M_Crt_Colossus_Hide` (tiling hide + faint crack emission);
* `M_Crt_Tentacle` (tiling tentacle skin, shared with the Mist Tentacles);
* `M_Crt_Colossus` (256 px detail atlas + emission for the eyes, core, plate seams and orifice lips).

**Scene:** `CharacterBody3D` "Colossus", containing:

* `Model` (the GLB; `Model/AnimationPlayer`);
* collision: body box, sac box, head box, four leg cylinders;
* `Markers/LookTarget`, `Markers/CoreTarget`, `Markers/StrikeImpact`;
* metadata: animation list, walk speed, strike impact time.

## Creature 02 - Mist Tentacles

These are appendages of something unseen that rise straight out of the mist. **There is no body.** The three variants
share one skeleton, `Root > T_01 ... T_10`, and one clip set. The same pose functions drive every variant, scaled by
its length, so code written for one variant drives the others.

| Variant | File | Length | Triangles | Shape |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Thin (searching) | `Mist_Tentacles/CRT_MistTentacle_Thin.glb` | 3.4 m | 883 | upright and wavy, dense ridges, a pale sensory bulb and two filaments at the tip, small sensory nodes along the underside |
| Medium (grasping) | `Mist_Tentacles/CRT_MistTentacle_Medium.glb` | 5.2 m | 1 219 | flattened underside lined with paired hooked barbs, tip curled into a hook |
| Large (aggressive) | `Mist_Tentacles/CRT_MistTentacle_Large.glb` | 7.8 m | 1 065 | hunched forward, overlapping plates down the back, a knuckled kink ringed with plates, hooked bone spike at the tip |

Every variant rises from a flared base that sinks into the mist. `Root` sits at the origin, on the mist surface.

**Clips** (24 fps keys):

| Clip | Length | Loop | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| `Emerge` | 2.6 s | no | rises from below the mist (Root from -1.05 x length to 0) while uncoiling; ends in the searching pose |
| `Search` | 6.0 s | yes | slow travelling-wave sweep with a twitching tip |
| `Coil` | 4.0 s | yes | coiled into a twisted spiral that slowly tightens and relaxes |
| `Reach` | 2.2 s | no | straightens and reaches forward (+Z); ends extended with the tip probing |
| `Strike` | 1.8 s | no | pulls back, then whips forward and down to the ground in front, then recovers |
| `Retract` | 2.2 s | no | coils and sinks below the mist |

`Search`, `Coil`, `Reach` and `Strike` pass the deterministic ground guard. `Emerge` and `Retract` pass below the
ground / mist surface on purpose.

**Scenes:** `Godot_Scenes/CRT_MistTentacle_{Thin,Medium,Large}.tscn`. Root `Node3D` with
`Godot_Scenes/veil_mist_tentacle.gd` (class `VeilMistTentacle`), containing:

* `Model`: the GLB (skeleton + `Model/AnimationPlayer`);
* `MistRoot`: a `Marker3D` at the mist surface, the placement root;
* `BoundsArea/Bounds`: a coarse static capsule (an `Area3D` with monitoring off).

Script API:

* `visibility` (0-1) and `fade_height` (metres) control how the tentacle shows. The script swaps the imported
  materials for `Shared_Materials/crt_mist_fade.gdshader`, which uses the same textures and dithers everything
  below the `MistRoot` height away. The dither is ordered (Bayer), with no transparency sorting and no screen /
  depth reads, so it is Compatibility-safe.
* `autoplay` (default `Search`).
* `play(clip, blend)`, `emerge()` (Emerge, then Search), `retract()`, `get_animation_player()`.

There is no damage, AI or targeting.

**Materials:** `M_Crt_Tentacle` (the tiling skin shared with the Colossus tentacles, 2 m repeat on these smaller
tentacles) + `M_Crt_MistTentacle` (128 px detail atlas: plates, barbs, spike, sensory bulb / nodes, base).

## Creature 03 - The Hound

`Hounds/CRT_Hound.glb` - 24 bones (+2 sockets), 3 286 triangles, one material (`M_Crt_Hound`, 256 px atlas with
faint emission on the sensory pits and spine seams). Scene: `Godot_Scenes/CRT_Hound.tscn`.

**Size:** about 1.0 m at the shoulder and 3.3 m from nose to tail tip. It keeps the established fast-predator role.

**Anatomy (canine-inspired, but wrong):**

* A low stance with the head carried below the shoulders. The shoulders are powerful and uneven (the right one is
  heavier), over a gaunt waist and a deep, keeled rib cage with rib ridges and a scar hollow.
* An unnatural spine: a double hump with a row of hooked vertebral spikes that grow toward the shoulders.
* A long, narrow, **eyeless** head. Rows of pale sensory pits run down the snout (3 left, 4 right), and two
  swept-back bone spines sit on the skull.
* A **split jaw**: the lower jaw is two separately hinged halves (`Jaw_L` / `Jaw_R`). They drop and spread sideways
  around a ridged maw (rows of teeth), with hooked fangs at each tip.
* Four digitigrade legs with backward elbow spurs, thin shanks and three long claws per foot. Sharp joints are
  rounded in the mesh so the inner creases do not fold.
* A thin whip tail (3 bones).

**Skeleton:**

* `Root > Hips > Spine1 > Spine2 > Chest > Neck > Head > Jaw_L / Jaw_R`
* `Tail_01-03` from `Hips`
* Legs `Leg_{FL,FR,HL,HR}_{Upper,Lower,Foot}`
* Sockets `Socket_Mouth`, `Socket_Chest`

**Clips** (30 fps keys, in place, feet placed by IK):

| Clip | Length | Loop | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| `Idle` | 3.0 s | yes | breathing, low sniffing head sway, jaw twitch, tail sway |
| `Walk` | 1.2 s | yes | stalking lateral-sequence walk, 0.7 m stride, 0.9 m/s, spine flex |
| `Run` | 0.45 s | yes | rotary gallop, 0.9 m stride, 5.0 m/s, strong spine flexion, head held forward |
| `Alert` | 1.4 s | no | crouches, spine arches, head snaps up and scans, jaw chatters, tail stiffens; ends held |
| `Attack_Bite` | 1.3 s | no | crouch, lunge (+0.32 m) with the forefeet stepping in, split jaw gapes about 42 degrees, snaps shut at 0.66 s |
| `Hit_React` | 0.8 s | no | sideways jolt, spine twist, head recoil |
| `Death` | 1.8 s | no | stagger, then the legs splay and the body drops and rolls; head and tail fall limp |

A ground guard lifts the head and tail so they never pass through the ground.

**Scene:** a `CharacterBody3D` with `Model`, a body box and a head box, `Markers/LookTarget` and `Markers/BiteReach`.
Its metadata holds the walk / run speeds.

## Validation

`Documentation/VALIDATION_REPORT.md` is the latest validator output. It covers:

* GLB integrity and topology: accessor ranges, unit normals, no degenerate or inside-out triangles.
* UVs: atlas UVs stay inside 0..1.
* Skin: joint hierarchy equals the manifest, identity rest, inverse bind matrices, normalised 4-weight skinning.
* Textures: embedded textures equal the PNG files; palette lock; sparse glow-only emission masks; lossless
  imports; nearest filtering.
* Animation:
  * every clip present, with unit quaternions;
  * looping clips are seamless and do not pop at the seam;
  * every clip is re-sampled from the GLB and skinned on every key, checked against per-creature ground and
    stretch limits.
* Scenes and gallery wiring.
* The Khronos glTF-Validator.
* Godot 4.3:
  * headless import: bones, skinning, external materials, emission, loop modes, RESET, collision, every clip
    moving its probe bone, the mist shader override;
  * a Compatibility-renderer (OpenGL 3, xvfb) run that instantiates every scene and plays every clip.

**None of this proves visual quality.** No screenshots, renders, turntables or videos were made, and the look of the
models has not been visually verified.

## Limitations

* **Visual quality is unverified** (see above). Proportions, silhouettes and motion were designed numerically and
  checked only by the automated metrics above.
* **Stretch:** linear blend skinning stretches the outer side of tightly bent joints. Measured maxima are in the
  report; the largest are about 1.7x local edge stretch at:
  * the Hound's hind knee in `Run`;
  * the Colossus and Shellcrawler joints in `Death`;
  * the Shellcrawler's legs in `Crawl_Sideways`.
* **Collision** is coarse and static. It does not follow the animation, and there are no per-limb hitboxes.
* **Locomotion is in place** (no root motion). A future controller must move the body at the documented speed.
  Turning and slopes are not authored; the feet are planted on flat ground only.
* **Non-looping clips** start and end in poses that differ from the idle. They rely on `AnimationPlayer` blend times
  (the Mist Tentacle script uses 0.3 s).
* **Gallery:** the gallery shows each model in its rest pose. The browser viewer does not play the clips (the
  gallery code was not redesigned).
* **Texel density:** the Colossus hide and tentacles use 32 px/m instead of the characters' density.
* **Mist fade:** the Mist Tentacle fade shader was compiled and run in Godot 4.3 Compatibility, but its look (dither
  pattern, fade height) was not visually checked.
* **Emission:** emission strengths are set in the `.tres` materials (0.5-0.8) and are meant to be tuned in the
  game's real lighting / Veil states. They are not wired to `VeilAtmosphere`.
